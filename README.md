# TechNova Sales Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project is an interactive sales dashboard created to analyze
TechNova's sales performance, revenue, profit, orders, products,
cities, and sales representatives.

The dashboard provides a clear view of overall business performance
and helps identify important sales trends and top-performing areas.

## Key Performance Indicators

The dashboard includes the following KPIs:

- Total Orders: 458
- Total Profit: $10.22M
- Total Quantity: 2.50K
- Total Revenue: $40.86M
- Average Revenue per Order: $89.22K

## Dashboard Analysis

### 1. Orders by Category

The dashboard compares total orders between:

- Electronics: 251 orders (55%)
- Accessories: 207 orders (45%)

### 2. Revenue by Month

The monthly revenue chart shows revenue trends from
January to December and helps identify months with higher
and lower revenue performance.

### 3. Orders by City

The dashboard analyzes total orders across different cities,
including:

- Bengaluru
- Hyderabad
- Delhi
- Chennai
- Pune
- Bangalore
- Mumbai
- Other/Unknown locations

### 4. Profit by Product

The dashboard compares profit contribution across different
products, including:

- Laptop
- Tablet
- Printer
- Monitor
- Keyboard
- Headphones
- Mouse

The total profit shown in the product analysis is $10.22M.

### 5. Top 3 Sales Representatives

The dashboard identifies the top three sales representatives
based on total profit:

- Ravi
- Kiran
- Sneha

## Dashboard Filters

The dashboard provides interactive filters for:

- Quarter
- Payment Mode

These filters allow users to explore the sales data based on
different business conditions.

## Key Insights

- Electronics generated a higher share of orders than Accessories.
- Monthly revenue varies throughout the year.
- Mumbai, Bangalore, Pune, Chennai, and Delhi show higher order
  volumes compared with several other cities.
- Product-wise profit contribution can be compared using the
  interactive donut chart.
- The dashboard highlights the top-performing sales representatives.
